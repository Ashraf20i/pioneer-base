# Demo video script: 30 seconds

**One decision and its consequence:** the Moon night is coming and the player has no reactor. The base goes dark system by system, and the debrief shows the chain.

**Rules for the video.** Screen recording of the real game only. English captions burned in (or an .srt file) for every spoken or written line. No music with lyrics, no NASA logo, no people on camera, no minors. Record at 1920x1080, browser zoom 110%, game on the Moon. Total length: 30 seconds or less.

**Before you record.** Open the game, press "Start Moon mission", and dismiss the hints. Practise this plan: on day 1 build **3 solar arrays and nothing else**, keep everything switched on, and press Run day quickly until the night starts (day 11). Then record the real run. If the game ends on a different day, adjust the shot timings, not the story. Rehearse twice. Use the same browser window size for every shot.
